The Charming Red Panda

Native to the eastern Himalayas and southwestern China, the red panda is an arboreal mammal slightly larger than a domestic cat. Despite sharing a name with the giant panda, they belong to their own distinct taxonomic family and are more closely related to raccoons and weasels.

They are famous for their fiery rust-colored fur, long ringed tails used for balance and warmth, and semi-retractable claws that make them agile tree-climbers.

Quick Facts

  • Habitat: Temperate mountain forests with heavy bamboo understories at altitudes of 4,900 to 13,000 feet.
  • Diet: Specialized herbivores consuming up to 20 pounds of bamboo daily, supplemented with berries, blossoms, and occasionally eggs.
  • Conservation Status: Endangered, facing population decline due to habitat fragmentation, deforestation, and poaching.
